It all started on November 27th, 2023...

Risman emerged from the intersection of a vital need for creative research environments and a personal aspiration to build an unprecedented intellectual space. Our philosophy centers on transforming educational limitations into opportunities for creative and scientific synergy.

Fields of Focus

Risman brings together perspectives from five core areas of psychology:
✧ Cognitive Psychology
✧ Child & Adolescent Psychology
✧ Personality Psychology
✧ General Psychology
✧ Philosophy of Psychology
Through articles, reflections, and discussions drawn from these fields, we aim to explore the many dimensions of the human mind and behavior.
